Customer Security Advisory

Dear Valued Customer,

To securely locate, investigate, and resolve your failed transaction, our support team will request only the first 4 and last 6 digits of your card number.

We strictly adhere to consumer protection and data security rules mandated by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP):

  • Legally Mandated for Investigations: Under BSP Memorandum No. M-2021-059 (supported by NPC Advisory Opinion No. 2021-026), sharing partial, masked details is an industry standard to track payment anomalies safely.
  • Completely Masked & Safe: A partial card number cannot process unauthorized purchases without the missing middle digits, expiration date, or security CVV code.
  • Identifies Payment Logs: The first 4 digits identify your card network and issuing bank; the last 6 digits allow the payment gateway to pinpoint the exact error log.
🔒 Our Golden Rule for Your Safety

We will NEVER ask for, and you should NEVER share: Full 16 digits, Expiry Date, 3-digit CVV/CVC, or OTP/PIN.
